Biscuit's sunny morning walk

From Auntie Maya · Saturday morning

Biscuit met me at the door with the whole back half wagging, ready before I had my shoes on. We headed out for the Cedar St loop while the morning was still cool and the sidewalks were quiet.

He sniffed every mailbox like it was breaking news, said a polite hello to the spaniel from the blue house, and then found his favorite sunbeam by the corner garden. He flopped right down and soaked it in for a good few minutes. I did not have the heart to rush him.

Back home, water bowl topped up and one treat earned for excellent leash manners. He was snoozing on his bed before I made it to the gate. A very good, very golden morning.
