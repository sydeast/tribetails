Biscuit led the whole loop today ❤

🐶
From Auntie Maya 30 MIN WALK · CEDAR ST LOOP · TODAY, 9:14 AM

Biscuit was already at the door doing his little spin before I had the leash off the hook. We took the long way down Cedar St so he could check every mailbox (he has opinions about all of them) and he greeted the corgi from number 14 with a full tail wag and a polite sniff.

About halfway through he found a warm patch of sidewalk and decided that was the spot for a sit and a think. I let him have a minute, then we carried on past the bakery, where he gave the door a very hopeful look. No luck, buddy, not today.

Back home he drank a good amount of water, earned his peanut butter treat, and was snoring on the cool kitchen tile before I locked up. A happy, tired boy. Doors locked, blinds left how you like them.
