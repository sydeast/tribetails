# Price Justification Script — App & Classified Leads

For when a lead asks some version of "so 30 minutes is $25?" on Yelp, Rover, Wag, Care.com, etc. Don't flinch, don't discount. Answer with confidence and reframe cost as value.

---

## The Message

Yes, ma'am. I'm a solo owner, so your pup gets my absolute undivided attention for the full 30 minutes, not a rotating cast of unknowns. And I show up when I say I will, on time, every time, and you'll be notified every step of the way.

Tribe Tails' rates wrap in over 10 years of professional animal care, including a decade fostering senior hospice dogs, so there's very little I haven't handled. You also get KinTales visit reports with photos and GPS tracking, and the peace of mind that I'm background checked, fully insured, bonded, and certified in pet first aid and CPR.

I get to learn your baby's preferences and quirks, and pay attention to any little changes that might mean something's off. It's about stepping in as family on your busiest days, so your pup never feels the difference. Is there anything else I can answer for you?

---

## Swap-ins

- **"ma'am"** — drop or adjust to fit the lead.
- **"the full 30 minutes"** — match whatever visit length they asked about.
- **"on your busiest days"** — works for recurring walks. For a leaving-town/vacation job, swap back to a "while you're away" version.
- **Business name** — on classified platforms (petsitter.com, Sittercity, Care.com), don't name Tribe Tails. Reword "Tribe Tails' rates" to "my rates" and introduce as Syd.

## Why it's built this way

- Opens and closes on feeling; credentials sit in the middle where they reassure without dominating.
- The close hands the conversation back warmly, no pressure.
- It spends a lot of your best ammo at once. That's right for a price objection, but if the lead goes quiet after, re-engage with a fresh angle (free meet & greet, a photo, a check-in) rather than repeating credentials.

## Back-pocket next step

If she replies "no, that's everything," offer the free meet & greet as the natural, low-pressure close. Keep it in reserve rather than firing it now.
