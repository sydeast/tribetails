## The exploratory review: all 18 screens, live production

The headline is not that error handling is bad. **The same codebase contains the
best and the worst error handling in the product.** Form Schemas names the failing
callable, offers Retry, and refuses to claim zero. Home renders "Open bookings: 0
/ needs a reply" while the read is permission-denied. Not a knowledge gap: every
screen hand-rolls its load states and about half get it wrong. That is a missing
component, which is why the rebuild fixes it rather than a patch.

Biggest findings (full detail in the plan doc):

- **AO-18, worst of the session.** `nowIso()` is UTC on web and LOCAL on desktop.
  One expect/actual, 18 call sites. Business is Austin (UTC-5), so after 19:00
  local the web admin's "today" is TOMORROW: invoices due today render OVERDUE
  five hours early EVERY EVENING, and Auntie Time (the day-of view) shows the
  wrong day. Root cause is one function doing two jobs: `completedAt = nowIso()`
  is correct (storage wants UTC), "what day is it for the operator" wants local.
  **Do not fix wasm's nowIso to local**, that starts writing local times into a
  UTC Firestore history. Split it. **Check Android, which is permanent.**
- **AO-12.** AuntieOS says PAID on an unredeemed $2000 CREDIT; the kinfolk's
  MyTribe portal says CREDIT on the same invoice. AuntieOS infers paid by
  negation; the portal enumerates states. Two apps, same money question, opposite
  answers. The single clearest argument for A1's shared package.
- **AO-19.** `serviceTone` substring-matches "sit" inside "vi-SIT-_60", so every
  visit paints house-sitting purple. Survived because a code comment documents the
  opposite, confidently.
- **AO-15.** The wasm canvas exposes NO accessibility tree. No screen reader
  support, and no element-level automation.

Also logged: AO-11 (directory cards hide pets, the "+N more" chip is itself
