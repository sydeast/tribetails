fix(form-schemas): the list reads again after the editor saves (#1615)

The web Form Schemas route opened the editor over the list and closed it on
save, but the list only loaded on mount, so a new or edited schema did not
appear until the operator pressed Reload. FormSchemas now takes a reloadKey and
FormSchemasView bumps it from onSaved. Cancel does not reload.

Android and the desktop console were read for the same defect and do not have
it: their list screen leaves composition while the editor is open and reloads
when it returns, and both editors confirm a save through their toast host.

Tests: FormSchemasView.test.tsx covers the reload after save and the confirmation
staying on screen, plus a single load on mount.
